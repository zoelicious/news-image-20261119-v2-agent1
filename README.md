# The World in One Day — 19 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page summing up Thursday 19 November 2026 in a
single image: 39 stories from 6 continents, from the Trump–Xi–Putin meeting
in Shenzhen and Anthropic's record $81.3bn IPO to Voyager 1 reaching one
light-day from Earth.

`main.py` (stdlib only) copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` (1024×1024) into
the output directory. The image itself was drawn at build time with a
flat-vector renderer; every story on it was reported on 19 November 2026.
